Gambling and betting sites across six UK regions, 2010 to 2026

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland each have their own devolved government, and London has its own mayor and assembly. The charts below show how many gambling and betting sites were operating in each of these regions every year from 2010 to 2026. Each chart has its own scale, since the regions differ a lot in size, so the lines are best read for their direction and not compared by height. More research would be needed to link the fall in gambling sites to devolution, if there is any link at all.
