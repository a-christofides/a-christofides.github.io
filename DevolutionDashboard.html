Gambling and betting sites across six UK regions, 2010 to 2026

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have their own devolved governments, and London has its own mayor and assembly. Each chart shows how many sites in the gambling and betting industry operated in that region each year. Note that each chart has its own scale, because the regions differ a lot in size.

Source: Office for National Statistics, UK Business Counts (local units), SIC 92000 Gambling and betting activities, accessed via Nomis, March of each year. These count sites, not betting activity or spending.
